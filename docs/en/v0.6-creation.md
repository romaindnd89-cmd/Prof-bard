# v0.6 · Creation (done and tested in game on 18 October 2026)

*English translation of [`../v0.6-creation.md`](../v0.6-creation.md) (French).*

> **Update (v1.0).** The improvisation window gained an "Instrument" menu and an **expert mode** with a full piano keyboard (G3 to C6, 30 notes). The language button is now "FR | US". See [v1.0-prestige.md](v1.0-prestige.md).

## Improvising
- "Improv" button of the songbook: eight keys (A S D F G H J K) play the scale of a mode (major, minor, dorian, phrygian, mixolydian, pentatonic) from a starting note,
  with the instrument in hand. No score and no chant. Nearby players hear it (`HITS` / `HEAR`).

## Composing
- In improvisation: "Record", play, "Stop", "Compose", a title. The addon sends the notes (time since the first, in ms) in pieces (`COMPBEGIN`, `COMPN`, `COMPEND`).
- The server decides everything (`BardeCompose.cpp`): at least 16 notes and 20 seconds, at most 240 seconds, notes between 55 and 84, notes less than 60 ms apart merged, placed on a grid of sixteenth notes at 84 BPM,
  tier from the density (notes per second: 1.2 / 1.8 / 2.4 / 3.0) without exceeding what the skill allows. At most 10 compositions per player. Title of 3 to 40 characters.
- Table `mod_barde_composition` (characters). The song id is 1,000,000 + id.
- **A composition gives no chant, no malus, no skill and no ranking**; it needs no skill and no instrument tier. It is played for pleasure, score and concerts.

## Scrolls
- "Copy" (on one of your compositions): 1 Light Parchment makes a Song Scroll (item 1950040). The song is inscribed as a permanent enchantment of the item.
- The scroll can be sold at the auction house or traded. Reading it teaches the song to the reader (table `mod_barde_char_learned`), plays a small learning effect and consumes the scroll.
  A song already known is not read. The scroll's tooltip shows the title, the composer and the tier (asked from the server through `SCROLL` / `SCROLLINFO`).
- If the composer deletes their composition, it disappears for everyone who learned it. A learned song is forgotten with "Delete".

## Songbook
- Groups: the five tiers of the book, "My creations" (your compositions), "Custom" (songs learned from scrolls).

## Bilingual addon
- "FR | US" button of the songbook, or `/barde lang [fr|en]`; see `docs/en/v0.5-scene.md`.
