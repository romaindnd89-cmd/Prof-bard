# v0.4 · Workshop

*English translation of [`../v0.4-atelier.md`](../v0.4-atelier.md) (French).*

> **Update (v1.0).** The sound-volume sliders were removed from the songbook, the workshop and the stage (replaced by a "Mute other bards" box). The money bonus at a camp (+10 % tips) is now off by default (`Barde.Camp.TipPercent = 0`): the camp now gives its listeners a share of the chant. The "Common instrument" tooltip line and the special materials were never added. See [v1.0-prestige.md](v1.0-prestige.md).

## v0.4a: tiers and crafting (done and tested in game on 17 October 2026)
- Ten instruments x five tiers (copper, silver, mithril, thorium, arcanite) = 50 items: `1950000 + 1000 x tier + instrument number`.
- "Workshop" window (songbook button or `/barde atelier`): tiers, required skill, materials with what the player has, "Craft" button.
  The server checks the profession, the skill, the materials and the bag space (`Atelier::Craft`).
- Recipes (`tools/tiers.json`): copper given (skill 1); silver 60: 2 silver bars, 2 wool, 2 medium leather; mithril 120: 3 mithril bars,
  2 mageweave, 2 heavy leather; thorium 180: 3 thorium bars, 3 runecloth, 2 thick leather; arcanite 240: 2 arcanite bars, 4 runecloth, 4 thick leather.
- A song of tier t needs an instrument of tier t or better; the instrument in hand is the best tier owned.
- GM commands: `.barde instruments [tier]`, `.barde competence <value>`.

## v0.4b: qualities (done and tested in game on 17 October 2026)
- Five qualities: common x1, uncommon x1.05, rare x1.10, epic x1.15, legendary x1.20 (quests only, not given yet at the time). The quality is a
  "permanent enchantment" with no effect (ids 1950501 to 1950504 in `SpellItemEnchantment.dbc`, server and client) that the game shows in the
  tooltip; `Atelier::QualityOf` reads it back, `Atelier::QualityMultiplier` multiplies the chant's base, and the result shows "(quality x1.1)".
- Draw at crafting (brief): without a gem 60 / 30 / 10 % (common / uncommon / rare); with the tier's gem 10 / 50 / 40; with the gem and the
  Arcane Crystal (arcanite) 0 / 25 / 50 / 25 (up to epic). Skill bonus: up to 10 points from common toward uncommon (one per 15 skill
  above the tier's).
- Gems: Malachite (copper), Jade (silver), Citrine (mithril), Star Ruby (thorium), Azerothian Diamond (arcanite). "With a gem" box in the workshop.
- `.barde instruments [tier] [quality]` gives instruments of a chosen quality, for testing.

## v0.4c: tuning (done and tested in game on 17 October 2026)
- Tuning is the item's **durability** (100 at the start), which the game shows in the tooltip. Each song played outside training lowers it by 4 (3 for the
  drum). The legendary never goes out of tune. Under 50 %, the chant is multiplied by 0.85 to 1 (linear); the result says "(out of tune x0.93)".
- **Tuning key** (item 1950020): "Tuning" tab of the workshop, "Craft 5 keys" button (1 copper bar and 1 coarse thread), then "Tune" per instrument
  (consumes one key, back to 100). The quality odds show in the tier tabs, with or without a gem.
- Migration (`mod_barde_characters.sql`): instruments from before tuning existed, at durability 0, are set to 100 once (`mod_barde_migration`).
- Volume: a "Sound effects volume" slider (a game setting) in the workshop, the songbook and under the stage (removed in v1.0).

## v0.4d: bard camp (done; free tuning tested in game on 18 October 2026)
- Kit crafted at the workshop (Tuning tab): 4 Wool Cloth, 2 Medium Leather, 1 Flint and Tinder (item 1950030, bundle-of-wood icon). Using it sets a **campfire**
  (object 1950401, a cooking-fire type like the world's "Campfires": you cook within 10 m) and **two benches** (1950402, to sit on) for 30 minutes.
  One camp per zone, one per bard per hour; no dungeon or battleground. The camp disappears when the server restarts.
- A bard's instruments within 8 m of the fire regain 1 % tuning every 5 s (tested). Players within 8 m who listen to a bard playing there receive 15 % of the
  chant for 10 minutes (to test with a second character; the share was later set to 50 %). Settings: `Barde.Camp.*`.
- The concert tips (+10 % at a camp) come with the purse of v0.5.
