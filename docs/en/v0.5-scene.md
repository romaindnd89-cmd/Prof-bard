# v0.5 · The stage

*English translation of [`../v0.5-scene.md`](../v0.5-scene.md) (French).*

> **Update (v1.0).** Applause no longer raises the tips: the money bonuses (+2 % per applauding spectator, +10 % at a camp) are off by default (`Barde.Concert.ApplausePercent`, `ApplauseMax` and `Barde.Camp.TipPercent` are 0). An applause is now an **ovation**: it lengthens the listeners' chant by 60 s (up to +5 min per concert) and counts for the bard's titles. The language button is now "FR | US". See [v1.0-prestige.md](v1.0-prestige.md).

## 0.5a Song mastery (done and shown in game on 18 October 2026)
- Bronze after 5 successes (+1 % to the chant), silver after 15 (+2 %), gold after 40 (+3 %). The songbook shows it under the best score; the result details it
  ("bronze mastery x1.01"). Thresholds in `Session::MasteryTier` (server) and `ns.MASTERY` (addon).

## 0.5b Ranking (done)
- "Ranking" button of the songbook: the five best scores on the server for the chosen song (`TOP`). Player titles come with prestige (v1.0).

## 0.5c Concert and purse (done; purse set tested in game, tips to be tested)
- A bard who plays (outside training) at a camp or in a rest zone (tavern, city) gives a concert: a purse (item 1950403, script `go_barde_purse`) appears at their feet.
  Spectators leave 10 silver, 1 gold or 5 gold (purse menu) or applaud (menu or `/applaudir`); the bard clicks to collect. If not collected, the purse is sent by
  mail 3 minutes after the last song (never more than 30 minutes).
- Bonuses (created money, adjustable; **off by default since v1.0**): +2 % per applauding spectator (max +20 %), +10 % at a camp. `Barde.Concert.*`, `Barde.Camp.TipPercent`.
- Good mood: in a tavern, players within 15 m receive 25 % of the chant; at a camp, 50 %.
- Deliberately not done: the brief's NPC donations (they would create unlimited money).

## 0.5d Duels (done and tested in game on 18 October 2026)
- "Duel" button of the songbook: select another bard, pick a song. They accept; both play at the same time. The best score wins (a gap under 0.5 %: a draw),
  the tips of both purses go to the winner (shared on a draw). Abandoning: the opponent wins.

## Addon language (French / English)
- "FR | US" button at the top of the songbook (it was "FR | en" at the bottom in this version), or `/barde lang [fr|en]`: the interface changes language and reloads. The choice is kept (`BardeDB.lang`); French is the default.
- All addon texts go through `ns.T()` (`Locale.lua`), which looks the translation up in `Locale_data.lua`; that file is generated by `tools/addon_translate.py` from
  `tools/addon_translations.py` (the dictionary to complete). Names sent by the server (songs, instruments, chants, effects, tiers) are translated by `ns.N()`, song subtitles by `ns.Sub()`.
- Messages sent by the server (refusals, camp, purse, duel...) stay in French: the server is not translated.
