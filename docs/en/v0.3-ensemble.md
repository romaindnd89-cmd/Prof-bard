# v0.3 · Ensemble: plan

*English translation of [`../v0.3-ensemble.md`](../v0.3-ensemble.md) (French).*

> **Update (v1.0).** A bard preparation window now opens when an ensemble is proposed: one card per bard, then a 5-second countdown before the song starts (`Barde.Ensemble.CountdownSeconds`). See [v1.0-prestige.md](v1.0-prestige.md).

Decision of 17 October 2026: **solo play buffs only the bard; the group buff is earned by playing together.**

## Principle
- Duo (two bards) x1.6, trio (three) x2. Cap: x1.2 of the base times the multiplier (design brief, section 6).
- Each bard brings their instrument's chant to the whole group. Different instruments: the chants stack. Same instrument: the strongest stays.
- One chant per bard. Maximum number of different chants received by one person: **3 (proposal, to be confirmed)**.
- Group accuracy = average of the bards. Tier requirement: each bard must own an instrument of at least the song's tier.

## Proposed flow
1. The group leader opens the songbook, picks a song and clicks "Together" (instead of "Play").
2. The server creates a session and sends `INVITE` to the group members who have the profession, the song unlocked and an instrument. They accept or refuse in a small window.
3. When all participants have accepted, the server sends `BEGIN` to each with the same start (server time + countdown); everybody plays their part.
4. Each client sends back its result (`RES`); the server waits for all results, computes the average accuracy and applies the chants to the whole group.
5. No result after the song's length plus a delay: the missing participant counts as absent (no multiplier for them).

## Parts (design brief, section 6)
| Part | Instrument | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Melody | the chosen instrument (gives the buff) | the main tune |
| Rhythm | drum, 4 skins | strong beats and fills |
| Harmony | fiddle (harp if the melody is on the fiddle) | counter-melody a fifth below the melody |

## Decisions (17 October 2026)
1. **Drum**: the 10th instrument (rhythm part) will be added; the timpani keeps its role (stamina).
2. **Rhythm and harmony parts**: generated automatically from the melody (not written song by song).
3. **Different chants on one person**: 3 at most (`Barde.Buff.MaxChants`).
4. **Stacking with class buffs**: the chant adds on top (no `spell_group`), so the profession stays worthwhile.

## The bard's presence (17 October 2026)
- During a song: a hidden aura (spell 1950307, a blue circle with a note) surrounds the bard, who keeps the instrument in hand;
  it disappears at the end or on abandon. A single `/me` line "starts playing ..." at the start, at most once every 30 s.
- The six other looks tried (1950301 to 1950306) show nothing in this client; look 2 (1950302) makes the character **dance**
  and put the instrument away: do not use it. Settings: `Barde.PlayAura`, `Barde.PlaySoundAura`, `Barde.PlayEmote`.

## Listening and dancing (17 October 2026, tested in game)
- **Listening**: the notes of a playing bard (solo or in an ensemble) are sent to the nearby players, whose addon plays the sound with the instrument
  (`HITS` from the bard, `HEAR^instrument^notes^kind` to the others; kind 1: ensemble partner, 0: plain listener). Range `Barde.ListenRange`
  (7.5 m), at most `Barde.ListenMax` (8) listeners. A player turns listening off with `/barde ecoute` (setting kept in `BardeDB`). A player
  who is playing hears only their partners.
- **Dancing**: for each song, a dance is drawn at random from `Barde.PlayEmoteState` (10, 400, 413: all three keep the instrument in hand,
  thanks to the client patch that removes the "put the weapon away" flag from dance animations 69 and 211 in `AnimationData.dbc`).
- Test GM commands: `.barde duo`, `.barde aura`, `.barde pose`, and `/barde auto` on the addon side.
