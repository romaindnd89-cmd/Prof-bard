# mod-barde v1.0: prestige

*English translation of [`../v1.0-prestige.md`](../v1.0-prestige.md) (French).*

This version adds what rewards a bard who keeps playing: ovations, titles, specialties, the legendary instrument, the piano, the profession guide.

## Ovations

A spectator can applaud near a concert's purse (`/applaud`, or `/applaudir` in French, or the purse menu).

- A spectator applauds only once per concert, never from the bard's own account.
- An applause lengthens the concert listeners' chant by 60 s (up to 300 s more per concert) and counts as one of the bard's ovations.
- The money bonuses (applause, camp) are 0 by default: a bard's money is the players' tips, which they receive in full.

Options: `Barde.Ovation.Seconds` (60), `Barde.Ovation.MaxSeconds` (300).

## Titles

"ReuseNN" rows of `CharTitles.dbc` that the game leaves free (ids 226, 227, 231 to 235), named by `tools/barde_dbc.py` in the server's file and in `patch-ZO.MPQ`. `BardeTitles.cpp` grants them.

| Title | Condition |
|---|---|
| the Minstrel | win 10 different songs of the book |
| the Composer | compose a song |
| Golden Voice | receive 5 gold in tips |
| Tavern King | win 10 duels (one win per opponent per day) |
| the Virtuoso | gold mastery (40 successes) on 3 songs |
| the Idol | receive 100 ovations |
| Legendary Bard | forge the legendary instrument |

The counters are in `mod_barde_char_stats` (ovations, tips, duels won) and `mod_barde_duel_log`. Titles are checked after a song, a composition, a duel, a chosen specialty and at every login.

## Specialties

From skill 180, the "Specialty" button of the songbook. The first choice is free, changing it costs 10 gold. No specialty gives money. Code: `BardeSpec.cpp`.

- **War Bard**: +10 % on the chants of the crumhorn, fiddle, piano, organ, ocarina, timpani and drum.
- **Court Minstrel**: range 7.5 m to 11 m, 16 listeners instead of 8, audience share x1.5 (camp 75 %, tavern 37 %), an applause counts double (+2 min, 2 ovations).
- **Storyteller**: +10 % on the lute, harp and flute chants; audience share x2 (camp 100 %, tavern 50 %).

Options: `Barde.Spec.MinSkill` (180), `Barde.Spec.ChangeCost` (100000 copper), `Barde.Spec.ChantPercent` (10). The specialty is stored in `mod_barde_char_stats.spec`. Protocol: `SPEC`, `SPECSET^n`, reply `SPEC^current^skill^cost`.

## The legendary instrument (the Quest of a Thousand Voices)

One per character, for ever, soulbound: it cannot be traded, sold or bought, and there is no other way to get one. It gives a chant x1.2, never goes out of tune, and the "Legendary Bard" title. Code: `BardeLegend.cpp`, window `addon/Barde/Legend.lua` ("Legendary quest" button at the bottom of the Workshop).

Five trials:

1. gold mastery (40 successes) **and** a 100 % run on all 40 songs of the book;
2. hand in 10 arcanite bars, 5 essences of water and 1 living ember;
3. beat a virtuoso in a duel (the title, or gold on 3 songs);
4. play a Grand Bard song in a trio of three real bards, 100 % each;
5. forge: the epic-quality arcanite instrument the bard chooses becomes legendary.

Progress is in `mod_barde_legend` (materials handed in, duel, trio, forged). Protocol: `LEGEND`, `LEGENDGIVE`, `LEGENDFORGE^instrumentId`.

For testing, a GM can validate the steps: `.barde legende tout` (songs, duel, trio; the materials are handed in in game) and `.barde legende reset` (clears the steps; an instrument already forged stays legendary).

## The piano

The psaltery is replaced by a **piano** (key `piano`, look of item 44924), with a synthesized piano sound (`tools/gen_instrument_sounds.py`). It keeps the magic-resistance chant.

## Improvisation

- An "Instrument" menu picks the instrument among those you own.
- Expert mode is a full keyboard from G3 to C6 (30 notes): Z X C V B N M, A S D F G H J K L, Q W, Shift for sharps.

## The guide and the songbook settings

- A "Help" button opens a profession guide from A to Z (`Help.lua`), in French and English.
- The volume bar is replaced by the "Mute other bards" box.
- The play aura is removed at login (it used to be saved with the character).

## Ensemble preparation

When a bard proposes a song to their group, an "Ensemble preparation" window (`Ensemble.lua`) opens for each participant: the song, the multiplier (duo x1.6, trio x2), and one card per bard with the icon of their chant, a badge (hourglass: has not answered, check: plays, cross: does not play), their instrument and their part.

- When everybody has answered, a **countdown** (`Barde.Ensemble.CountdownSeconds`, 5 s by default, 0 to start immediately) gives time to get ready, then the window closes and the song starts.
- At the end, the window comes back with everybody's accuracy, the group's, and whether the chant is given, then closes by itself.
- Protocol: `ENSTATE^id^phase^song^bards^average‰^chant^members^multiplier‰^countdown ms` (phase 0 waiting, 4 countdown, 1 playing, 2 over, 3 cancelled).
- The virtual partner of `.barde duo <song> [accuracy %]` also goes through the preparation.

## Installation

`BUILD_BARDE.bat` then `INSTALL_BARDE.bat`. New addon files (`Help.lua`, `Legend.lua`, `Ensemble.lua`) require fully quitting the game before restarting it: a `/reload` does not re-read the `.toc` list.
